Analysts

line = how fresh the view is

Four small programs. Each one watches a group of currencies against the US dollar, decides from fixed rules whether it is bullish or bearish, and plays its own instrument. No AI makes these calls.

    On stage: K kills fx, R brings it back.

    What these buttons do

    Each analyst is its own program. They talk to each other directly, with no central server, so the desk keeps working when one fails.

    Kill
    Stops that analyst's program, with no warning. After about 3 s of silence the next analyst in the ring (fx → havens → commodity → contrarian → fx) takes over its slice. You hear the slice move to the coverer's instrument and the status says "covering".
    Degrade
    Throws away 75% of the analyst's messages, like a bad connection. Its freshness line fades and its part drifts into reverb. Press again to heal.
    Revive
    Restarts the analyst. It takes its slice back at the next bar line.

    Killing fx doesn't change the 2020 card. Killing havens or commodity does: the analyst that covers reads the slice its own way, so risk comes out different (havens: card on 28 Feb; commodity: no card).
